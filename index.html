How to Form Teams

8 students form 2 teams of 4. The two students with the highest MAP scores become Team Captains. Captains compete in a quick challenge (dice roll, coin flip, or mental math duel). The winner chooses first pick. Teams alternate selecting members until teams are full.

Objective

Be the first team to reach 10 points. Main Dungeons are worth 1 point each. Training Grounds are worth 0.5 points each.

How the Game Is Played

  • Both teams work simultaneously on any dungeon of their choice
  • Each team chooses which member attempts each dungeon
  • 85% or higher = Dungeon Cleared! Record it using the team color buttons
  • A dungeon claimed by one team cannot be claimed by the other
  • At any moment, the teacher may randomly choose a team member to explain the math concept or solve a sample problem aloud on the board
  • Correct explanation = dungeon progress counts
  • Incorrect explanation = team must pause and retry later

Additional Rules

  • Team members may not coach during explanations
  • Everyone must be prepared — any adventurer may be called
  • Already-cleared dungeons cannot be replayed
  • Strategy matters: choose wisely who enters which dungeon
